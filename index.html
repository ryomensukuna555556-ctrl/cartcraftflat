Our story

CartCraft started in 2016 in a two-room workshop, built on one stubborn idea: everyday objects deserve the same care as luxury ones. No trend-chasing, no disposable batches — just materials chosen to outlast the people who buy them.

A decade later, that idea hasn't changed. Every product in this catalog is still designed in-house, tested well past the point of reasonable patience, and priced without the usual markup games.

  1. 2016

    Founded in a small workshop with a single leather-goods line and a waiting list of forty people.

  2. 2019

    Opened our first flagship store and expanded into audio and wearables.

  3. 2022

    Moved to fully traceable materials and a lifetime-repair policy across the catalog.

  4. 2026

    Took the full collection online — the store you're shopping right now.
